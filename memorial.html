Kigali Genocide Memorial

A place of remembrance and learning.

The Kigali City Council commissioned the development of this memorial site, where up to 250,000 genocide victims were buried in mass graves, into a permanent memorial centre and exhibition. Today, the site is managed by the Aegis Trust, which continues to expand its role as a place of education and reflection for survivors and young people.

Highlights

  1. Exhibition halls documenting Rwanda’s history
  2. Peaceful memorial gardens
  3. Educational programs for youth

Visitor Tips

  • Wear respectful attire
  • Photography restrictions apply
  • Guided tours available
