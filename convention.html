Kigali Convention Centre

A premier venue for international and local events.

The Kigali Convention Center (KCC) is a multipurpose building designed for hosting conferences, exhibitions, meetings, and social gatherings. It serves as one of Rwanda’s premier venues for international and local events. The building is notable for its large dome design, which is symbolic and draws attention for its unique look.

The Convention Center is more than just a building; it is part of a larger complex. This includes the adjacent Radisson Blu Hotel, making it convenient for event attendees who need accommodations. The site is strategically positioned to reflect Rwanda’s growing focus on hospitality, business, and infrastructure.
